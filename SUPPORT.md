# Support: Pocket Outpost Tactics

**Need help or found a bug?** [Open a support request](https://github.com/surproductions/Pocket-Outpost-Tactics/issues/new). Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the mission you were playing.

## How to play
- **Move:** tap one of your units, then a blue tile.
- **Attack:** tap a red target to preview the exact damage, then tap it again (or press **Attack!**).
- **Abilities:** Shield Wall (Guardian), Precision Shot (Ranger), Dash (Scout), Field Kit (Engineer). Each has a cooldown.
- **Damage:** attack minus defense minus cover, minimum 1. No dice.
- **Enemy reach:** tap an enemy to see every tile it can strike next turn.
- **Stars:** win, lose no one, and finish within the round target.

## FAQ
- **Does it need internet?** No, all missions work offline.
- **New phone?** Progress is part of your normal iPhone backup and is restored with it.
- **Restore the Supporter Pack?** Settings or Banners → **Restore Purchases**.
- **Faster battles?** Turn on **Fast enemy turns** in Settings or the pause menu.
- **Motion sensitivity?** Turn on **Reduce Motion** in Settings (it also follows the iOS setting).
