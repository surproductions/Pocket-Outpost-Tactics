# Privacy Policy: Pocket Outpost Tactics

_Effective October 22, 2026_

## The short version
**Pocket Outpost Tactics does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data.** The game works fully offline. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no user accounts.

## Information stored on your device
To save your progress, the game keeps a small file on your iPhone containing your outpost, squad levels, mission stars, settings, cosmetic choices, and simple gameplay counters (for example, battles won and the days you played). This file never leaves your device, except as part of your own iCloud or device backups if you have enabled them, which are governed by Apple's privacy policy. You can delete it at any time with **Settings → Reset Progress** in the game, or by deleting the app.

## Purchases
The optional, cosmetic-only Supporter Pack is sold through Apple's App Store. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The game only receives a confirmation from Apple that the purchase is active so it can unlock the banner, and it can restore it through **Restore Purchases**.

## Tracking
The game does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

## Network use
The game makes no network requests of its own. The only connections are those made by Apple's StoreKit framework when you open the shop, buy, or restore a purchase, and opening this policy or the support page in your browser.

## Children
The game is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
