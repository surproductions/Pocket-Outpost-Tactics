Command a tiny squad. Outthink a whole warband.

Pocket Outpost Tactics is a bite-sized turn-based strategy game you can play in 3 to 5 minutes. Lead a hand-painted miniature squad across a tabletop diorama battlefield, protect your frontier outpost from raiders, and grow it from a lonely tent into a thriving stronghold.

SMART, READABLE TACTICS
• A compact 7×9 grid built for one-handed portrait play
• Exact damage previews: no dice, no hidden rolls, just good decisions
• Use forest cover, high ground, barricades and flanking to turn the fight
• Tap any enemy to see exactly where it can strike next turn

FOUR DISTINCT HEROES
• Guardian: tough frontliner whose Shield Wall protects nearby allies
• Ranger: precise marksman who strikes from 2–3 tiles away
• Scout: lightning-fast flanker who dashes in and out
• Engineer: builds barricades on the fly and patches up allies and the gate

AN OUTPOST THAT GROWS WITH YOU
Win supplies and timber, then upgrade your Command Center, Watchtower, Barracks, Workshop and Supply Depot. Every upgrade visibly transforms your outpost and makes your squad stronger.

A CAMPAIGN ACROSS THE FRONTIER
• Greenwood Frontier: 10 handcrafted missions that teach every trick
• Dustfall Canyon, Frostwatch Pass and Ember Ruins: new terrain, veteran raiders and warlord bosses
• Defend-the-gate and eliminate-the-raiders objectives
• Three-star ratings for every mission
• A new Daily Skirmish every day

PLAYER-FRIENDLY BY DESIGN
• Plays fully offline
• No ads, no energy timers, no loot boxes
• Save & resume any battle, even after a phone call
• Haptics, Reduce Motion support and fast enemy turns

An optional, cosmetic-only Supporter Pack is available for players who want to support the game. Nothing for sale affects combat.
