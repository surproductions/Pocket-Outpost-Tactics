# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-22
First release.
- Turn-based tactics on a 7×9 square grid with deterministic damage, cover, high ground, flanking and barricades.
- Squad classes: Guardian (Shield Wall), Ranger (Precision Shot), Scout (Dash), Engineer (Field Kit).
- Enemies: Raider, Raider Archer, Heavy Brute, and Thornjaw the Warlord, driven by a utility-scoring AI.
- Campaign: 10 handcrafted Greenwood Frontier missions plus 15 seeded missions across Dustfall Canyon, Frostwatch Pass and Ember Ruins; Eliminate and Defend objectives; 3-star ratings.
- Outpost: Command Center, Watchtower, Barracks, Workshop and Supply Depot, with visible upgrade transformations.
- Economy and progression: coins, supplies, timber and squad XP (levels 1–5).
- Daily Skirmish, save & resume, synthesized sound effects, haptics, Reduce Motion and fast enemy turns.
- Cosmetic banners (coins) and an optional cosmetic Supporter Pack (StoreKit 2).
