How to play

  • Move: tap one of your units, then a blue tile.
  • Attack: tap a red target to preview the exact damage, then tap it again (or press Attack!).
  • Abilities: each class has one ability with a cooldown: Shield Wall, Precision Shot, Dash, Field Kit.
  • Damage: attack minus defense minus cover, minimum 1. There are no dice, so what you see is what you get.
  • Enemy reach: tap an enemy to see every tile it can strike next turn.
  • Stars: win, lose no one, and finish within the round target.

Frequently asked questions

Does the game need an internet connection? No. All missions work offline.

I changed phones. How do I keep my progress? Progress is included in your regular iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) and restored with it.

How do I restore my Supporter Pack? Open Settings or Banners and tap Restore Purchases.

Can I make battles faster? Turn on Fast enemy turns in Settings or the pause menu.

Motion sensitivity? Turn on Reduce Motion in the game's Settings (it also follows the iOS Reduce Motion setting).
